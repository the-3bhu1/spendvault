SpendVault Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · Applies to the SpendVault app for Android (package com.spendvault.app)

In short
  • SpendVault has no accounts and no servers. Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • We don't collect, sell or share your data, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • SMS are checked on your phone. Messages that aren't bank alerts are dropped straight away, and your inbox is never read.
  • Data leaves your phone only for optional features you turn on, and only to the service named for that feature.

SpendVault ("the app", "we") is a personal finance tracker built by Tribhuvan Komarla. This policy explains what the app does with your information. If something here is unclear, email tribhuvankomarla@gmail.com.

1. What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter or the app creates for you is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. The developer cannot see it, and there is no copy on any server. This includes:

  • Your finances: transactions, accounts and balances, categories, budgets, tags, bills, group splits, money lent or borrowed, investments and reward points.
  • Card details you choose to save: cardholder name, card number, expiry date, network and issuing bank. A card's CVV is also encrypted with a key that's generated inside your phone's secure hardware (the Android Keystore). That key can't be exported, so a CVV can only ever be read on the phone where you entered it.
  • Your profile: your name and an optional profile photo.
  • App lock: if you set a PIN, only a one-way hash of it is stored, never the PIN itself. If you use fingerprint or face unlock, Android does the check, and the app only learns whether it passed. Your biometric data never reaches the app.
  • Ask Vault conversations and your settings.
  • Your own API keys (Google Gemini, logo.dev), if you add them. The Gemini key is kept in the Android Keystore.

2. SMS (Auto-Log SMS, optional)

If you turn on Auto-Log SMS, the app asks for Android's permission to receive SMS. It explains what follows and asks you to agree before Android shows its own permission prompt. Once you agree:

  • The app receives each new incoming SMS, including while it's closed, and checks on your phone whether it's a bank, card or UPI transaction alert.
  • Messages that aren't transaction alerts, such as personal messages and OTPs, are dropped immediately. They're never stored or sent anywhere.
  • A detected alert waits on your phone until you add it to your ledger or dismiss it. The entry you add keeps only the amount, merchant, account or card digits and date, never the message itself.
  • The app never reads messages already in your inbox, and it never sends SMS.
  • SMS are never uploaded to the developer or shared with anyone. The one exception is the optional AI SMS filter (section 4), which sends a detected alert's text to Google Gemini.

You can turn Auto-Log SMS off at any time in Settings, or revoke the SMS permission in Android's settings.

3. Other phone features

  • NFC (tap to add a card): when you hold a card to your phone, the app reads its number, expiry and network on the phone and fills them into the form. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: used only to alert you to a transaction detected from an SMS.
  • Files: the app reads or writes a file only when you export a backup, import one, or attach a contract note.

4. Optional features that use the internet

SpendVault works offline. The features below connect to a third-party service only when you use them. The request goes directly from your phone to that service, never through us. Like any internet request, the service can see your IP address. Its own privacy policy covers what it does with the request.

5. Backups and emails

  • Backups you export: a backup file or code contains your financial data so you can restore it later. It goes wherever you choose to save or share it, and it isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. Backups never include card CVVs, your PIN or your API keys.
  • Android device backup: if you've turned on backup to your Google account, Android may include the app's data in it. That's controlled by Android and your Google account settings. CVVs in such a backup stay encrypted and can't be read on another phone.
  • Emails to us: if you email support, or use "Report" on an Ask Vault answer (which opens your email app with the question and answer filled in), we receive only what you choose to send. We use it only to reply and to improve the app.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't collect or receive your personal or financial data.
  • We don't sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no tracking.
  • You don't need an account to use the app.

7. Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your phone, you control it completely:

  • Settings → Wipe Data erases your finances, card details, profile, settings, pending SMS detections and Ask Vault conversations. Your API keys stay until you remove them in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone.
  • Remove any backup files you exported, and delete any emails you sent us if you want those gone too. We'll delete our copy of an email on request.

We hold no data about you on any server, so there's nothing else to delete.

8. Security

Your data is kept in storage that only SpendVault can access on your phone, behind an optional PIN or biometric lock, and CVVs are also encrypted with a hardware-backed key. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we recommend a screen lock on your phone and keeping exported backups private.

9. Children

SpendVault is meant for adults managing their own finances. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone.
